Fixed dropped requests during deployments for every service on the shared chart.

2024–2026 · multiple clusters, 15+ services

Field Notes

Three problems I ran into, and what fixed them.

Topology-aware routing has minimum requirements

It needs at least three ready endpoints in each zone, and nodes must have zone labels. If not, Kubernetes accepts the setting but ignores it. Check the EndpointSlice to confirm it works.

You cannot change DynamoDB billing mode on a replicated table

Remove the replicas, change the mode, then add the replicas back. Terraform manages both in one resource, so this takes three separate applies.

KEDA with schedule and metric triggers uses the highest value

The schedule sets the minimum, and metrics scale above it. Min and max replicas still apply. KEDA also overwrites any manual edits to the HPA it manages.
