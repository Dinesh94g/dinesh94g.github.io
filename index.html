About

I build and run the infrastructure behind a live commercial real-estate auction platform — Kubernetes, AWS, and the delivery pipelines that put code in front of bidders.

Most of my work is migrations that cannot afford downtime: a fifteen-service language re-platform, an ingress replacement across production clusters, a node-provisioning swap. I care about making risky changes cheaply reversible, and about proving a fix worked with data rather than asserting it.

Ingress Modernisation — NGINX to HAProxy

Infrastructure lead · 2026 · Multi-cluster, adopted beyond my own team

Designed a zero-downtime migration in which both ingress controllers share a single load balancer, so rollback is a replica scale — under a minute, with no infrastructure apply and no DNS change. Packaged as versioned Terraform modules and a self-service runbook that three teams outside my own adopted. Delivered to production as part of a major cluster version upgrade.

Eliminated deployment-time client errors along the way, verified by controlled load testing rather than assumed from the absence of alerts.

Kubernetes Cost & Capacity Optimisation

Infrastructure lead · 2024–2026 · 15+ services

Introduced hybrid autoscaling — scheduled scaling for predictable load, metric-driven scaling as the safety net — including custom scheduling around a recurring business-event cycle. Right-sized CPU and memory across the fleet from production telemetry, and reduced cross-zone data transfer costs with topology-aware routing.

Cut off-hours non-production compute cost by roughly two thirds. Notably, I reverted planned savings on several business-critical services before merge once load analysis showed peak-event demand needed the headroom.
